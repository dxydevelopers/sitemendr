# Service Plan: Custom Website Development

## **Objective**
To deliver high-end, uniquely architected websites for service businesses and complex brands, combining AI efficiency with expert human craftsmanship.

---

## **The 5-Step Journey**

### **Step 1: Assessment (Project Scoping)**
*   **Action**: Client completes the "Professional Architecture" questionnaire.
*   **Focus**: Business goals, required technical integrations, and brand style.
*   **AI Role**: Determining the optimal technical stack and feature list.

### **Step 2: Discovery (Architecture & Roadmap)**
*   **Dashboard View**: **"The Architect's Preview"**
*   **What the client sees**: 
    *   Sitemap and technical architecture overview.
    *   Design mood-boards and high-fidelity homepage concept.
*   **Exit Gate**: Client clicks **"Approve Design Direction"**.

### **Step 3: Consultation (Detailed Briefing)**
*   **Dashboard View**: **"The Project Briefing Desk"**
*   **Key Actions**: 
    *   Upload brand assets (Logo, Photos, Brand Guidelines).
    *   **Expert Touch**: Mandatory or optional consultation with a Senior Developer/Project Manager.
    *   Finalize the "Technical Specification Document."
*   **Exit Gate**: Signing the **Project Contract** and initial payment.

### **Step 4: Building (Development & Iteration)**
*   **Action**: Human developers build the site using the AI foundation.
*   **Dashboard View**: **"Production Roadmap"**
*   **Features**: Feedback loops where clients can review and comment on a "staging" version of the site.

### **Step 5: Launch (Go-Live & Handover)**
*   **Action**: Final QA, browser testing, and server optimization.
*   **Dashboard View**: **"Owner's Manual"**
*   **Post-Launch Tools**: Training videos, content editor access, and performance analytics.

---

## **Key Dashboard Features (Creative View)**
*   **Milestone Tracker**: Visual timeline of build phases.
*   **Feedback Portal**: Integrated tool for pointing and commenting on designs.
*   **Asset Library**: Centralized storage for all project media and documents.
*   **Revision History**: Logs of what was changed and why.
