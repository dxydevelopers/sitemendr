# Service Plan: AI-Powered Dropshipping Program

## **Objective**
To empower entrepreneurs to launch high-performance e-commerce stores using AI-driven automation, strategic niche analysis, and expert technical foundations.

---

## **The 5-Step Journey**

### **Step 1: Assessment (Strategic Foundation)**
*   **Action**: Client fills out the "E-commerce & Niche" questionnaire.
*   **Focus**: Target audience, product categories, and brand personality.
*   **AI Role**: Analyzing market trends based on the selected niche.

### **Step 2: Discovery (The Store Vision)**
*   **Dashboard View**: **"The Merchant Preview"**
*   **What the client sees**: 
    *   AI-generated homepage concept and product layout.
    *   Suggested inventory categories and messaging strategy.
*   **Exit Gate**: Client clicks **"Confirm Vision"** to move to technical setup.

### **Step 3: Consultation (Operational Setup)**
*   **Dashboard View**: **"The Store Configuration Desk"**
*   **Key Actions**: 
    *   Select niche-specific features (e.g., product reviews, abandoned cart recovery).
    *   Configure basic shipping/tax preferences.
    *   **Expert Touch**: Optional 15-min strategy session to review the "Product-Market Fit."
*   **Exit Gate**: Activation of the **Dropshipping Subscription**.

### **Step 4: Building (Automated Generation)**
*   **Action**: Sitemendr AI generates the full store structure, SEO metadata, and essential integrations.
*   **Dashboard View**: **"Build Progress Tracker"**
*   **Status Indicators**: "Generating Product Pages," "Configuring Payment Gateway," "Optimizing Mobile UI."

### **Step 5: Launch (The Grand Opening)**
*   **Action**: Final QA, domain connection, and handover.
*   **Dashboard View**: **"Merchant Command Center"**
*   **Post-Launch Tools**: Live sales tracking (simulated/integrated), inventory health, and marketing suggestions.

---

## **Key Dashboard Features (Merchant View)**
*   **Niche Insights**: AI-driven suggestions for trending products.
*   **Sales Simulator/Tracker**: Visual data on store traffic and conversions.
*   **Integration Manager**: One-click connection to dropshipping suppliers.
*   **SEO Automation**: Periodic reports on search engine rankings.
