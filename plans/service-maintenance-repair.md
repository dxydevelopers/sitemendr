# Service Plan: Technical Repair & Maintenance

## **Objective**
To provide reliable, proactive protection and rapid technical support for existing websites, ensuring 99.9% uptime and peak performance.

---

## **The 5-Step Journey**

### **Step 1: Assessment (Site Diagnosis)**
*   **Action**: Client provides site URL and describes existing issues.
*   **Focus**: Identifying bugs, speed bottlenecks, or security vulnerabilities.
*   **AI Role**: Automated performance audit and security scan.

### **Step 2: Discovery (The Audit Report)**
*   **Dashboard View**: **"The Diagnostic Report"**
*   **What the client sees**: 
    *   A "Health Score" for their existing site.
    *   List of prioritized fixes (Critical vs. Recommended).
*   **Exit Gate**: Client clicks **"Authorize Fix Plan"**.

### **Step 3: Consultation (Service Plan Selection)**
*   **Dashboard View**: **"The Protection Desk"**
*   **Key Actions**: 
    *   Choose between "One-Time Fix" or "Monthly Peace-of-Mind" plans.
    *   Provide site access (FTP/CMS credentials) via a secure vault.
*   **Exit Gate**: Activation of the **Maintenance Subscription**.

### **Step 4: Building (Repair & Hardening)**
*   **Action**: Technical team resolves issues and implements security patches.
*   **Dashboard View**: **"Technical Activity Log"**
*   **Status Indicators**: "Applying Security Patches," "Optimizing Database," "Resolving Javascript Conflicts."

### **Step 5: Launch (Verification & Monitoring)**
*   **Action**: Re-running the audit to prove success.
*   **Dashboard View**: **"Security & Health Monitor"**
*   **Post-Launch Tools**: 24/7 Uptime alerts, monthly health reports, and priority support ticket access.

---

## **Key Dashboard Features (Technical View)**
*   **Health Dashboard**: Live status of uptime and security.
*   **Support Ticket System**: Direct line to technical responders.
*   **Update Manager**: Logs of automated and manual core updates.
*   **Secure Credential Vault**: Encryption-protected storage for site access.
